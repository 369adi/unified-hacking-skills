Each upstream project is distributed under its own license. This aggregation does **not** relicense any of the original content — each source's original terms continue to apply to the material taken from it. Fused skill files preserve each source verbatim and clearly label its origin (both in the merged `SKILL.md` banners and under `_sources/`).

If you are an upstream author and want attribution adjusted or your content removed, please open an issue.
